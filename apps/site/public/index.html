What the panel shows

The month, at a glance

The menu bar shows this month so far, or today if you prefer. It updates a few seconds after your tools write to their logs.

What your plan is worth

Tell clai you pay for Claude Max, ChatGPT Pro or Google AI Pro and it shows the API-priced value of what you used, as a multiple of the price.

Where it went

This month by tool and by model, and a 30-day chart that reads out any day you point at.

The pace and the limits

Where the month is heading, and how much of your Claude and Codex 5-hour and weekly limits you have used. Claude's limits count claude.ai and cloud sessions too.

History that stays

Claude Code deletes transcripts after 30 days. clai keeps its own totals, so last month is still there next month.

Prices that keep up

Fast mode, cache reads and writes, long prompts and price changes are priced by the day. New models reach the app the day they are added to this site.

Nothing to sign up for. Your usage stays on your Mac.

clai has no account and sends no telemetry. It reads the files your tools already write and keeps its totals in ~/Library/Application Support/clai.

  • Reads token counts, model names and times, nothing else.
  • Never reads your prompts, your code or the replies you get, and never looks in Desktop, Documents, Downloads or iCloud.
  • Prices every request at the vendor's API list price, labelled API-equivalent.
  • Talks to the internet once a day, to fetch the price list from this site and ask GitHub for a newer clai. You can turn that off.
  • Reads your organization's usage report from Anthropic every 30 minutes, only if you connect one with a key. Nothing about your usage is ever sent.

Installing

The first time you open clai

clai is not notarized by Apple yet, so macOS says it cannot check clai for malicious software. Click Done, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to the message about clai and click Open Anyway, then confirm. macOS remembers the choice.

The Terminal install above avoids the prompt because files that curl downloads are not marked as coming from the internet. It checks the download against the release's SHA-256 checksum before installing.

Where did clai go?

clai lives in the menu bar, as the tally at the top right of the screen; the first launch also opens its panel in a window. On a MacBook with a full menu bar, macOS can hide menu bar items behind the camera notch, and macOS 26 can keep them out of the menu bar (System Settings → Menu Bar). Opening clai again from Applications, Spotlight or Launchpad always brings its window back. Quit with ⌘Q while the window is open, or right-click the tally and choose Quit clai.

How does clai work out the cost?

Every request in the logs has its token counts: new input, cached input, cache writes and output. clai prices them at the vendor's API list price on the day the request ran, including fast mode, long-prompt tiers and web searches, and labels the result API-equivalent. With a subscription you pay the plan, not this; the figure tells you what the same work would cost on the API. The price list is on this site, and the app fetches it once a day.

Does clai count claude.ai, ChatGPT and cloud sessions?

Claude's, through Anthropic's two official sources, both off until you turn them on in Settings. Claude plan limits show how much of your Pro or Max 5-hour and weekly limits you have used, and they count everything: claude.ai, the Claude apps, cloud sessions and Claude Code. Claude Code hands them to its status line; clai becomes that status line command, runs yours as before, and puts it back when you turn the switch off. For a Claude Console or Claude Enterprise organization, paste an Admin or Analytics API key and clai prices the organization's claude.ai chats, cloud sessions, Cowork, Claude Design, Slack and API usage from Anthropic's usage report, without counting this Mac's Claude Code twice.

Individual Pro and Max plans have no usage report, so their chats and cloud sessions cannot be priced in dollars. ChatGPT and Gemini on the web are not counted: their vendors offer no usage API for consumer plans.

Apple silicon or Intel?

Both: one download runs natively on every Mac with macOS 13 Ventura or later.

Checking the download

Every release lists SHA-256 checksums on GitHub. In Terminal: shasum -a 256 ~/Downloads/clai-mac.dmg

Updating clai

clai checks once a day and shows a notice in its panel when a new version is out. Run the Terminal command again, which quits clai and replaces it, or quit clai first (right-click the tally and choose Quit clai) and then drag the new copy into Applications: macOS does not replace an app that is running. Your totals carry over.

Coming from clai 0.4? It kept its data in ~/.clai, which the new app does not use, so that folder can go.

Removing clai

Right-click the tally and choose Quit clai, drag clai from Applications to the Trash, and delete ~/Library/Application Support/clai if you also want its totals gone.
