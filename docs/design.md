# Design

clai is an instrument, not a brochure: a real number, where it came from, nothing decorative. These rules cover the Mac app and getclai.vercel.app.

1. **The hero is a number with its basis.** The month's figure leads the panel, labelled "API-equivalent at list prices". Every cost says how it was worked out: a model priced like a family member says "priced like …", a tool's own figure says "cost as logged", a model without a price shows "–", never $0.
2. **Apple-native.** System materials and SF Pro, figures in monospaced digits. The panel is 360 pt wide. Platters are a 4.5 % fill with continuous corners (16 pt for the month card, 10 to 12 pt for platters, 6 to 8 pt for controls).
3. **One accent.** Ledger green `#1E7A62` (dark `#4FBE9C`); the month card goes from `#1E7A62` to `#0E5444` with white text. Amber `#9A6D00`/`#D9A441` means warning and red `#A83A3A`/`#E06C6C` critical (limit meters at 70 % and 90 %). Tools have their own colours, used for dots and share bars only.
4. **Motion only for change.** Nothing loops, floats or counts up; bars and figures change when the numbers do. The site fades sections in once; reduced motion turns that off.
5. **Copy is specific.** Say what happens in the user's words ("Reading the logs your AI tools keep on this Mac…"). No "seamless", "supercharge", "AI-powered".
6. **The menu bar figure.** The month so far by default (the panel's headline). Today or the icon alone in Settings. Before the first usage the tally stands alone. The figure is written only when it changes.
7. **Say what leaves the Mac.** The footer says "Usage data stays on this Mac"; Settings says what the daily online check asks for, and it can be turned off.
8. **Always a way in and out.** A window on first launch and on reopen, Quit in the right-click menu and with ⌘Q, a note when clai runs from Downloads or a disk image.
9. **Both themes designed**, contrast checked; controls have accessibility labels; the chart has a spoken summary.
10. **Sample data is labelled.** Screenshots on the site come from `clai --render` (sample data) and say so.
